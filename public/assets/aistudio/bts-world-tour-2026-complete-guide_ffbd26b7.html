North America — the routing

The US leg covered 10 cities with stadium-scale stops including:

  • Los Angeles — four shows
  • East Rutherford, NJ — two MetLife Stadium nights
  • Baltimore — two nights at M&T Bank Stadium
  • Arlington, Texas — stadium stop
  • Las Vegas — Allegiant Stadium

Extra shows were added within a day of the initial on-sale — and sold out too. That double-sellout pattern (initial dates plus same-week additions) became the tour's signature everywhere it went.

Europe — the London crown

The European headline stop was Tottenham Hotspur Stadium, London, early July 2026: roughly 130,000 fans across the run, a venue attendance record, and BTS's first UK shows since 2019. The Guardian called it "pure joy and astonishing versatility"; the city itself spent a week lit in "Arirang red" with pop-ups, an Outernet fanzone and a Thames boat installation. Several European cities reportedly sold out in about an hour when tickets went live.

How tickets worked — and what's left

Face-value reality: North America, Europe and the first wave of Asia on-sales are gone. For any remaining dates, your only legitimate channels are:

  • Official promoter/tour pages — for production releases near showtime
  • Venue box offices — for returned or production-held seats
  • Authorised resale platforms — only the ones the local promoter designates

The scam checklist — read this before any purchase

  • No legitimate seller ever DMs you first. Every "I have extra tickets" message on Instagram/X/TikTok is a scam until proven otherwise.
  • "Cheaper than face value" from strangers = bait. Real resale runs at a premium on a tour this size.
  • No screenshots as proof. Ticket "confirmations" are trivially faked; only seller-side platform transfers count.
  • Pay protected only. Direct bank transfers and crypto = irreversible = goodbye money.
  • Hotel bundles: "ARIRANG packages" from unofficial sellers were already a problem in Kaohsiung — local authorities investigated hotel overcharging there in January.

If you can't get a ticket: the cinema route

This is the genuinely good option, not the consolation prize: the ARIRANG live-viewing program has broadcast the tour to roughly 6,100 cinemas worldwide. ODEON (UK) carried Goyang and Tokyo dates; Cineworld screened the Busan anniversary show; chains across Asia, Europe and the Americas have joined throughout the year. The production is stadium-scale, the crowd is full ARMY, and encore screenings keep being added. For most fans who missed out, this is how they'll have actually seen ARIRANG.
