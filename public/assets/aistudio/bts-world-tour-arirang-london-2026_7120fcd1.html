Whether you were there, missed out, or are trying to work out what official merchandise and "what's included" packages actually covered, here's the complete picture — as of November 10, 2026.

The ARIRANG London takeover — what the city actually got

The show itself was only half the event. The London stop came wrapped in a full "THE CITY ARIRANG — LONDON" activation that turned the capital into a BTS theme park for a week:

  • Official pop-up store — tour merchandise in central London, with queue systems and daily restocks.
  • Outernet fanzone — the giant-screen central London venue hosted fan programming around show days, free to enter.
  • Landmarks lit "Arirang red" — London's skyline joined the tour's signature color, plus light shows and a boat installation on the Thames.
  • Samsung Galaxy experiences — interactive photo zones and installations around the stadium district.
  • Nike x BTS ARIRANG collaboration — tour-branded apparel plus a Nike By You customization experience, one of the year's most sought-after sneaker-culture drops.

The lesson for future stops (Bangkok and Jakarta are next at the time of writing): the takeover matters. Fans who arrived two or three days early got the pop-ups, the photo ops and the fanzone — fans who flew in on show day morning mostly got a queue.

Merch and "pre-orders" — how it actually worked

Honesty time, because this is the part that trips everyone up. Official ARIRANG merchandise was sold through exactly two channels: in-person at tour venues and through official BTS/Weverse shop drops. "Pre-orders" in the strict sense applied mainly to limited-edition items announced before the London dates, which shipped after the shows.

What the official drops included, per announcements through the year:

  • Tour-branded apparel from the Nike collaboration (including the Nike By You pieces)
  • Vinyl and physical collectibles tied to the ARIRANG era
  • Photocard and lightstick-linked bundles at venue stores
  • City-specific items at the London pop-up that never hit the online store

Exact stock and allocations varied by city and dropped with little warning — fast followers on the official shop won most battles, everyone else learned about it on resale within the hour.

How to spot fake ARIRANG merch (this still matters)

Months after the run, fake "ARIRANG merch" listings are still circulating on social marketplaces. Before buying anything second-hand:

  • Check the seller trail. Official Weverse Shop orders show in your account history; screenshots of "order confirmations" are trivially faked.
  • Look for the authenticity marks. Official tour goods carry consistent tag printing, holograms where applicable, and quality that replica sellers rarely match at their price points.
  • Price check. If a "sold-out" Nike collab piece is offered near retail by a stranger with no feedback history, that's not luck — that's bait.
  • Pay protected. Never send direct bank transfers for resale goods; use platforms with buyer protection.

Can you still experience ARIRANG in 2026? Yes — three ways

1. Cinema live-viewings. The tour's live-viewing program reached roughly 6,100 cinemas worldwide — including ODEON in the UK for the Goyang and Tokyo dates and Cineworld screenings of the Busan anniversary show. Encore and replay screenings keep appearing; if you're UK-based, check ODEON and Cineworld listings before assuming you've missed everything.

2. The remaining tour legs. Asia is in full swing: Kaohsiung runs three November shows, Bangkok plays December, and Jakarta closes the year December 26–27 with an added third show. Latin America venues were announced in March.

3. Official streaming. Netflix carried the "BTS THE COMEBACK" live stream back in March, and recorded concert content has continued rolling out on official channels all year.

What reviewers actually said about the London shows

UK press was overwhelmingly euphoric: The Guardian called the nights "pure joy and astonishing versatility," Rolling Stone UK went with "dazzling stadium spectacle," and the BBC framed it as a genuine cultural event — the group's first UK performances in seven years, celebrated accordingly. The consistent themes across reviews: a massive production scaled to the moment, a setlist that stretched from classic-era hits through the ARIRANG-era material, and the surprise-song segment that rotated night to night.

When did BTS play London in 2026?

Early July 2026, at Tottenham Hotspur Stadium — their first UK shows since 2019, drawing around 130,000 fans across the run and setting a venue attendance record.

Where can I buy official ARIRANG merch now?

Official BTS/Weverse shop drops and authorised retailers only. Venue stock sold out during the run; avoid social-media sellers and lookalike "pre-order" pages entirely.

What did the Nike ARIRANG collaboration include?

Tour-branded Nike apparel plus a Nike By You customization experience, announced before the tour and sold through official channels — it became one of the most counterfeited items of the year, so buy protected.

What made ARIRANG significant beyond the numbers?

It was BTS's first world tour since all seven members completed mandatory military service — a comeback-scale production that BBC reporting around announcement projected could gross around $1 billion, placing it among the biggest tours ever mounted.

Nothing specific has been announced for the London nights as of November 10, 2026 — but the tour's cinema and streaming program has released recorded content all year, so official channels are the place to watch.
