A new six-member era — the honest context

This is the anniversary album's bigger frame: Mark departed NCT DREAM in 2026, and DREAM AFTER DREAM is the group's first full album as six. The transition was handled the fandom-first way — announced around the fan-meeting cycle, tested live at Incheon in August where the sold-out pre-sale answered any demand questions, and now sealed with an album that topped charts on day one.

And the leadership question that defined the era's opening weeks got its answer: Jeno steps in as the group's new leader as of November 10 — the six-member era now has its structure, its sound, and its sold-out receipts.

Why "Boogie Woogie" is the right title track

After ten years, the members chose a party over a requiem — and the details make it special:

  • Jaemin wrote lyrics. A member's pen on the anniversary single turns the celebration personal — the group narrating its own decade.
  • LEEJUNG choreographed it. The choreographer behind some of the group's most beloved moments reunited with NCT DREAM for the anniversary dance — announced November 5, five days of anticipation before the MV.
  • Disco funk over balladry. NCT DREAM's story has always been youth — and the 10-year mark is being marked with joy rather than nostalgia alone. "The Sun" handles the reflective half; "Boogie Woogie" throws the party.

The numbers on day one

Per Korean chart coverage on November 10, DREAM AFTER DREAM is topping both album and physical/digital charts as the comeback lands — the strongest possible opening for an anniversary album, and a clear statement that the six-member era starts with full force. The Seongsu pop-up is running for the album, and the champagne-green Seoul lighting campaign earlier in the year already set the celebratory stage citywide.

The members' own words

In the pre-release Q&A with 매일경제 (November 9), the members framed the decade simply: "The past 10 years have felt like a dream." DREAM AFTER DREAM — the title isn't abstract; it's the group's own summary of where ten years landed, and what comes after the dream: the second decade, six members, one new leader, and a disco party to open it.
